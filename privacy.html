Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026 · Bulbry (com.bulbry.home)

1. Summary

Bulbry is a local-first home lighting registry. Your fixtures, bulb labels, photos, notes, audits and replacement logs are stored on your device. Bulbry does not operate a user account system or a cloud database for your records.

2. Data stored on your device

  • Room names, fixture names, slot names and bulb label details you type or dictate.
  • Photos you take with the camera or choose from your photo library.
  • Speech-to-text notes created with the microphone button.
  • Replacement events, audit observations, archive snapshots and your in-app credit balance.

This data stays in the app sandbox until you choose to export it or delete it.

3. Permissions

Bulbry asks for permissions only when you use a related feature:

  • Camera – when you tap Take Photo or Snap Spec to photograph a nameplate or fixture.
  • Photo Library – when you tap Choose Photos, or Save to Photos to store a picture in your library.
  • Microphone & Speech Recognition – when you tap the Microphone button so spoken words become editable note text. Audio may be processed by Apple speech services according to Apple's policies.
  • Notifications – Bulbry shows the system notification prompt once when you enter the home tabs. Bulbry does not send push or local notifications.

You can change any permission later in iOS Settings.

4. In-app purchases

Credit packs are consumable in-app purchases processed by Apple through the App Store. Bulbry receives transaction confirmation from StoreKit and stores your resulting credit balance locally. We do not receive your payment card details. There is no restore purchases feature because packs are consumable credits.

5. Sharing and export

Bulbry does not upload your records. Exports (JSON, CSV, ZIP) are created only when you request them, and you decide where they are saved or shared through the iOS share sheet.

6. Analytics and advertising

Bulbry contains no third-party analytics, advertising SDKs or tracking pixels. Bulbry does not use App Tracking Transparency or IDFA.

7. Retention and deletion

You can purge records inside the app. Uninstalling Bulbry removes the local sandbox. Exports you already saved elsewhere are not deleted by the app.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: use the support channel listed with the App Store listing for Bulbry.
